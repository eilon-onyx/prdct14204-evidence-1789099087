public.app_definitions is the ONLY cross-tenant (shared) surface the merge could touch;
every reference table repointed by the migration lives inside the tenant schema
(onyx_security). The migration is invoked with TENANT_SCHEMAS=onyx_security, so its writes
are confined to that schema by construction.

-- INTERPRETATION
-- * This lab stack provisions exactly ONE tenant schema (onyx_security), so there is no second
--   tenant's data to read directly. The cross-tenant guarantee is instead proven at the ONLY
--   place a tenant-scoped merge could leak across tenants: the shared public.app_definitions
--   catalog. It is byte-identical (same sha256, empty diff) before and after the apply, so any
--   other tenant that keys off app_id 'python3' (or any other shared row) resolves exactly what
--   it resolved before -- the merge changed nothing they can see.
-- * This is enforced structurally, not incidentally: _IDENTITY_STRING_COLUMNS omits
--   public.app_definitions / app_definition_versions from the re-key sweep, and the migration is
--   scoped by TENANT_SCHEMAS. A merge cannot re-key or delete a shared row.
